No Surprises

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Union City HVAC provides honest, upfront pricing across Union City, covering everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a thorough assessment of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

A lot of HVAC failures send small signals well before the system stops. Watch for rising utility bills, weak airflow at the registers, frost on the copper lines, or a bedroom that never catches up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually pinhole refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. An early service call at the first sign often costs far less than a failed compressor.

Not every call is an emergency. Plenty of Union City homeowners ask us to size up an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, replacing on your own schedule beats scrambling for a new unit during a cold snap. We'll give you a straight answer on how much life your equipment has left.

Preventive Care

Maintenance Plans for Union City Homes

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service heads off failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still inexpensive to fix. At Union City HVAC, every plan is built around your home's specific equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check on the ductwork.

Most homes do best with two visits a year: a spring tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. The schedule flexes with the age of your equipment, how hard your household runs it, and local conditions like dust and pollen, which coat condenser coils and raise energy use fast.

Think of maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already paid for. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, and that lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Proper Sizing

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

Bigger isn't better when it comes to HVAC. An oversized unit short-cycles, wastes energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs nonstop and still falls behind on the hottest afternoons. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. We run a real load calculation that factors in your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Choosing new equipment comes down to more than a SEER2 number. We'll compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, walking through operating costs, electrical and venting work, and the installation itself. Families with a growing household frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds more even temperatures from room to room and uses less energy. The decision is yours, based on your home and budget.

Common Problems

The HVAC Repairs Union City Homes Need Most

Homes in Union City run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Union City HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and refrigerant recovered and recharged after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
