Complete Heating & Cooling Services for Los Angeles Properties

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Two common situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Keeping Your Los Angeles HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
