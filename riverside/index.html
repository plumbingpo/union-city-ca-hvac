Two common situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. Keeping Your Riverside HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a few miserable nights.
