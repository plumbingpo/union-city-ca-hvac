Warning Signs for HVAC Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. These are the signals our technicians see most often when a problem is underway:

  • The system short-cycling on and off without reaching the set temperature
  • Weak airflow from the supply registers throughout the house
  • Puddles near the furnace or air handler from a blocked drain line
  • A breaker that trips whenever the system tries to run
  • New noises such as grinding, squealing, buzzing, or banging
  • The outdoor unit humming but refusing to actually start
  • Vents blowing warm when you want cool, or cool when you want heat
  • Utility bills climbing while comfort keeps falling

HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Take new noises seriously; a squealing motor bearing is far cheaper before it seizes
  • Leave every supply and return vent open and free of furniture or rugs
  • During heat waves and cold snaps, set the thermostat a few degrees closer to the outdoor temperature to ease the load
  • Swap in a fresh filter every one to three months; a clogged one starves the whole system of air
  • Have the system professionally serviced twice a year, once before summer and once before winter
  • Give the condenser room to breathe by keeping plants, leaves, and clippings away from it
  • Run a vinegar flush through the condensate line each spring to prevent clogs and float switch trips
